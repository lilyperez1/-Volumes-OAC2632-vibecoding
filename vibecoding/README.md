Lily — Personal Website

How to view

- Open `index.html` in your browser (double-click or use a local server).

Where to add your images

- Place photos in `assets/images` and name them `cat1.jpg`, `cat2.jpg`, `cat3.jpg` to appear in the gallery.
- Replace the placeholder SVGs in `assets` with your illustrations.

Next steps

- Customize `contact.html` with your real email and social links.
- Replace placeholder images and tweak colors in `css/style.css` as desired.
